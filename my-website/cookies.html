Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

CineFlex may use cookies, local storage and cached files to keep you signed in, remember settings, save viewing progress and improve loading speed.

Your controls

You may clear app cache and histories in CineFlex Settings. You may also block or remove browser storage, although doing so can disable sign-in, PWA, watch progress and personalisation.

Third-party services

Embedded media, authentication, analytics or advertising providers may apply their own storage technologies and policies when their services are used.
