CineFlex FreeReels — Playable starter catalog
=============================================
This build includes playable open-license/public-domain-friendly starter media.

Included titles:
1) Big Buck Bunny — Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0. Split into 5 FreeReels-style episodes.
2) Elephants Dream — Blender Foundation open movie. Split into 4 FreeReels-style episodes.
3) Flower — Mozilla CC0 sample, lightweight test title.

Important:
- These are NOT copyrighted FreeReels-app series.
- Do not add or redistribute copyrighted short-drama episodes unless you have permission.
- Attribution/source links are shown inside the player.
- Video files are streamed from their source URLs; they are not bundled into this ZIP.
