Last updated: 30 July 2026

CineFlex respects intellectual-property rights and reviews complete copyright complaints in good faith.

Submitting a notice

Send a written request identifying the copyrighted work and the exact CineFlex URL or content entry being reported. Include your full name, contact details, a good-faith statement that the use is not authorised, and a statement that the information supplied is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised representative.

Review and action

Valid notices will be reviewed and access may be removed or disabled where appropriate. Incomplete or unclear notices may require additional information before action can be taken.
