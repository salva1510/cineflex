About CineFlex

Last updated: 30 July 2026

CineFlex is designed as a responsive entertainment discovery and viewing interface for phones, computers and television browsers.

Our focus

The project focuses on fast browsing, TV-remote navigation, personalised discovery, flexible themes, installable PWA support and clear tools for reporting problems.
