Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This policy explains the basic information CineFlex may store to operate the service.

Information stored

CineFlex may store account details supplied during sign-in, profile preferences, watch progress, watchlists, search history, theme settings, device/browser information and technical error data.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account features, resume playback, personalise recommendations, improve reliability, prevent abuse and answer support requests.

Local storage and cookies

The app may use browser storage, cookies and service-worker caches for sign-in, preferences, performance and PWA installation. You can clear compatible data from CineFlex Settings or your browser.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the Contact page.
